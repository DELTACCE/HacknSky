What we collect

Only what the registration form asks for: name, email, college, phone (optional), year, track preference, team details.

Why

To run selection, contact you about the event, arrange travel and safety, and tell you about changes.

Who sees it

The organising team and the institution’s staff who help run the event. We do not sell data.

Analytics

If enabled, we count page views and performance without cookies. We respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data through the contact page.

Retention

Registration data is deleted after the event unless you agree to hear about future editions.
