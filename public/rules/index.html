Rules

Draft rules Draft. Final rules are published before the open call.

Teams and eligibility

  • Teams have exactly two members. Nobody can be on more than one team.
  • Participants must be currently enrolled students at a recognised institution (to be confirmed by the organisers).
  • Twenty teams take part: five selected at Christ College and fifteen through the open call.

What you build

  • Start the project at the event. Bring ideas, not finished code.
  • Open-source libraries, public APIs and public datasets are allowed. List them in your project README.
  • Any code written before the event must be declared and shown in the repository history.
  • AI coding assistants are allowed if you say how you used them in the README.

Judging

  • On Day 2 at 10:00 every team demos to the jury. Five teams advance to the finale.
  • Teams are scored on the published criteria. The jury’s decision is final.
  • A working demo is expected. Slides alone do not qualify.

Ownership

  • Your team keeps ownership of what it builds.
  • The organisers may show demos, screenshots and team names to promote the event, with credit.

Safety

  • Follow instructions from staff around water, the falls and the cable car.
  • The ride is proposed for finalists and is subject to operational confirmation.
  • Anyone who endangers themselves or others may be asked to leave.

Disqualification

  • Plagiarism, submitting someone else’s work, breaking the code of conduct or tampering with other teams’ projects.

See also the code of conduct.
