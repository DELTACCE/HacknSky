FAQ

Quick answers. Anything marked to be confirmed will change.

What is HacknSky?

A two-day, overnight destination hackathon at Athirappilly Falls in Kerala, organised under DELTA × DATARA’27 by Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous), Dept. of CSE (Data Science).

When and where is it?

Proposed for 15–16 January 2027 at Athirappilly, Kerala. Dates and venue are subject to confirmation, and everyone who registers interest will be emailed if anything changes.

Who can take part?

Teams of exactly two students. Five teams are selected at Christ College and fifteen more through an open call for other institutions. The exact eligibility rules are in the draft rules.

Can I register alone?

Yes. Tick “I need a partner” on the form and we will try to match you with another solo participant. Matching is not guaranteed.

How are the twenty teams chosen?

Five from the campus round and fifteen from the open call, using a defined evaluation that will be published before the call opens.

Is there a fee?

To be confirmed. This page will be updated, and registrants will be told first.

How do I get there?

The proposed plan is buses from Christ College, Irinjalakuda, at 06:30 on Day 1. Travel for teams from other institutions will be confirmed. See the Venue page for distances.

Where will we sleep and eat?

The plan includes base-camp check-in with beds, chai, dinner and an overnight build. Exact arrangements will be confirmed in the participant guide.

What should I bring?

A laptop and charger, your college ID, a headlamp or torch for the night build, a water bottle and any hardware for your idea. A final list comes with the participant guide.

Is the cable-car ride guaranteed?

No. The ride is proposed for the finalist teams and is subject to operational confirmation.

What are the prizes?

The awards programme is still being planned. See the Prizes page.

What happens to my data?

Only what the registration form asks for is collected, for event communication. See the privacy notice.

Who do I contact?

See the Contact page. Contact details are published there once they are confirmed.
