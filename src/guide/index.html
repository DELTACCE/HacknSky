Participant guide

A two-day student hackathon in Athirappilly, Kerala. Here’s what to know before you apply.

Proposed: 15–16 Jan 2027 · Athirappilly, Kerala. Please wait for confirmation before booking travel.

Can I enter?

Students from Christ College of Engineering and other institutions can take part under the draft eligibility rules.

Do I need a team?

The proposed size is 4 students per team. Final team size and arrangements for students without a team are not confirmed.

How does selection work?

The plan is to select 5 teams on campus and 15 through an open call. Every team demos its project; 5 finalists present to the jury. See the draft judging criteria.

What happens on the day?

Check in, meet your mentors and build overnight. Day two is for demos and final presentations. Read the proposed schedule and travel notes.

What should I do next?

Check registration availability before submitting details. The FAQ covers equipment, fees and accommodation.
