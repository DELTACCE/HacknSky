Code of conduct

Draft Participant conduct and safety

HacknSky is for everyone to build and learn safely.

  • Be respectful. No harassment, discrimination or intimidation.
  • Credit other people’s work and respect the rules.
  • Look after the place: leave no litter and stay clear of restricted areas.
  • Follow staff instructions, especially around water and heights.
  • Report concerns to any organiser. Anyone breaking the code may be asked to leave.
