# Architecture
- **Build:** Vite multi-page. `world.js` (including React Three Fiber) and the separate Three.js chunk load lazily. The branded intro also loads lazily, so plain mode and info pages don't request either 3D scene or engine.
- **Boot:** `main.js` → `ui.js` (loader, HUD, sheets, focus trap) → dynamic `world.js` `startWorld()` (fonts, builds the world, mounts Fiber landmarks, compiles shaders, then enters).
- **Plain mode:** `?plain` or the “Plain page” button skips WebGL and shows the content as a normal page. WebGL failure falls back the same way.
- **World (`world.js`):** procedural terrain, river and waterfall sharing one water shader, instanced forests, lodge, summit deck, cable line with gondolas, totems, per-letter 3D captions, camera keyframes with a chase camera for the cable chapter. Toon shading with inverted-hull outlines.
- **Fiber landmarks (`src/js/valley/`):** `createRoot` mounts onto the valley's existing canvas, renderer, scene and camera; the scroll runtime manually advances it only when drawing. `Falls.jsx` layers a shader on the original curtain and batches sculpted foam, spray, ripples and riverbank stones. `Gondola.jsx` portals its details into each animated cabin and batches hardware so repeated details don't add a draw call apiece. The intro still has its own, temporary Three.js canvas.
- **Quality ladder:** frame windows of 30; if the average exceeds 24 ms it steps down: 1 forest outlines off · 2 trees 70% · 3 fireflies, grass decor, most outlines, waterfall streaks and airborne spray off; fewer foam meshes · 4 trees 45%, pixel ratio −0.2 · 5 render every other frame. Weak phones (≤4 GB or ≤4 cores) or “Graphics: Low” start at 5. Reduced motion freezes water flow and the cabins, and disables airborne spray. Phone DPR is budgeted to about 1 megapixel with a floor of 1.25; multisampling is off on phones at 1.5× and above.
- **Forms:** `form.js` posts to `formEndpoint` (see `backend/`), else redirects to `registerUrl`, else falls back to `mailto:`.
- **Telemetry:** `telemetry.js` is a no-op unless `analyticsEndpoint` is set; respects DNT/GPC; sends `perf`, `quality_tier`, `loaded`, `js_error`.
- **Pages:** `scripts/build-pages.mjs` renders `data/event.json` into `public/<slug>/index.html`.
